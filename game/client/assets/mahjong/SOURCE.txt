Aka dora (red five) faces
-------------------------
faces/0m.png, faces/0p.png, faces/0s.png are derived from faces/5m.png,
faces/5p.png and faces/5s.png by recoloring the ink to red. They are not
separate artwork: same geometry, same paper texture, same lighting, so the
red fives sit next to the other tiles without a style break.

Generated by: python3 game/tools/make_aka_tiles.py
(pass --check to verify the committed PNGs are up to date)

The derived images remain CC0 1.0, like the source artwork.
